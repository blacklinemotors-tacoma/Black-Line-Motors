Black Line Motors is an independent auto repair shop and mobile mechanic service located at 1002 E F St, Tacoma, WA — serving Federal Way, Lakewood, Puyallup, Auburn, Spanaway, Gig Harbor and surrounding areas within 30 miles. Common Questions

What cars do you work on in Tacoma?

Black Line Motors is an independent full-service auto repair shop in Tacoma, WA. We work on German, Asian and domestic vehicles: all makes and models.

Can you service my car without voiding the factory warranty?

Yes. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, you are not required to use a dealership for routine maintenance. Black Line Motors uses OEM-spec parts and documents all services, keeping your warranty intact.

Do you offer mobile mechanic service in Tacoma?

Yes, we offer mobile mechanic service within 30 miles of Tacoma, WA. We come to your home, work, or roadside. Call (253) 220-7390.
