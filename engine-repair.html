FAQ

Questions

How do I know my timing chain is bad?

A rattle on cold start, a check engine light with timing codes, or a rough idle are common signs. We confirm it before any work.

Do you replace engines?

Yes. We do engine replacements and swaps on many makes. We give you the price before we start.

Can you show me what is wrong?

Yes. We take photos or borescope video of your engine and show you the problem.

Do you work with extended warranties?

Yes. We accept extended warranties, service contracts and mechanical breakdown insurance for engine repairs.
