FAQ

Questions

How do I know I need new brakes?

Grinding or squealing, a shaking steering wheel when braking, a soft pedal or a longer stopping distance are common signs. Bring it in and we will check it and show you.

Do you replace rotors every time?

No. If your rotors are in good shape we tell you. We only replace what is worn.

Do you do wheel alignment?

We do not do alignment in our shop. After suspension work we tell you if an alignment is needed.

How long does a brake job take?

Most brake pad and rotor jobs are done the same day. Call (253) 204-9383 to book.
